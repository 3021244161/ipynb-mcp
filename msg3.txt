perf: make NDJSON framing linear (third attempt, and the first that is)

Three designs, two reviews, two of them quadratic, each for a different reason:

- v3 kept a chunk list and concatenated only when a line was emitted. That fixed
  the COPY but left the SCAN re-reading the whole newline-free prefix on every
  push (64 MiB in 16 KiB chunks: ~2.2 s and growing with chunk count).
- v4 added a scan cursor. The rescan went away, and two terms came into view:
  the per-push walk over the chunk list (the review counted 34 million chunk
  visits for that same input) and a `Buffer.concat` of the growing prefix
  (8.6 GB copied).
- This version holds the unterminated remainder in ONE buffer that grows by
  doubling, so each byte is copied at most twice over the line's whole life and
  scanned once. The invariant that keeps it small: the staging buffer never
  contains a newline, so every complete line either lies inside the incoming
  chunk or straddles the boundary.

I introduced and fixed two regressions on the way, both caught by the tests
rather than by reading:

1. allocating a fresh buffer per line was SLOWER than what it replaced (a 64 MiB
   payload of small lines allocated 65 537 buffers and took 27 s), so the buffer
   is reused and a separate length tracks the valid prefix;
2. `indexOf` with a `fromOffset` returns an ABSOLUTE index, and treating it as
   relative to the running offset made the loop treat every newline after the
   first as being at the end of the buffer.

The new `[NEW-3]` case asserts the ALGORITHM rather than the wall clock: it
counts the bytes the scanner inspects (<= 1.1x the payload) so a quadratic scan
cannot pass on a fast machine.

NEW-4 — `#norm` memoized. It runs `realpathSync` and every session lookup used to
call it once per session plus once for the query, so an N-cell run performed N
synchronous filesystem calls on the hot path. The memo is invalidated when the
session it came from is removed, since the path may resolve differently later.

Verified: 227 unit (1 skipped) + 44 integration green.
