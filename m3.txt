docs: register D-037 and correct two claims that did not match the measurements

DEVIATIONS.md gains D-037, which NARROWS D-032 rather than extending it: the gate
judges the cells a write is responsible for, takes nbformat's own leniency rules
as part of the rules, and reports carried-forward content as a warning. D-032's
intent (answer for the result) was right; its implementation made "the result"
mean "the whole input", which turned one historical quirk into a permanently
unwritable notebook. D-032 now points at D-037 for scope.

README's two timeout claims were wrong in ways the review measured:

- "also ends its kernel ... the next run rebuilds" hid that the shutdown is
  ASYNCHRONOUS: the response returns first and the process may live until the
  interrupted cell finishes on its own. No kernel is reported immediately and
  nothing is orphaned, but a reader deserves to know the CPU is not free yet.
- "prompt (timeout_seconds plus a few seconds)" is `timeout_seconds` + about
  10 s: measured 10.2 s for a 2 s timeout, because the interrupt grace and
  teardown are real work.

Also corrected: two comments citing the wrong review item and an unverifiable
"27 s" figure (it described an intermediate design that never existed in any
commit), and `#normCache` invalidation now matches on the normalized value so a
repainted symlink cannot leave a stale identity behind (MISC-3).

AGENTS §9 gains the two rules this round paid for: a guard must be able to name
the mutation that turns it red, and a refusal must be able to say whether the
condition it refuses was introduced by this operation or was already there.
